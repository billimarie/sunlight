# Cohort #1 - Planting Plan (DeepSeek)

Sunflowers are not just flowers in the high desert; they are **living infrastructure**. They are the first line of defense against the wind and the sun.

Placement is everything.

## The Wind

The bus points **North** (back to the South). This is crucial.
- **Prevailing Spring/Summer Winds:** **West/Southwest**. This wind is hot, dry, and carries abrasive sand.
- **The Bus Effect:** The bus creates a **Wind Shadow** on the **East** side, but it creates a **Venturi Tunnel** (accelerated wind) around the **corners**, especially the **Northwest** corner where the wind hits the bus face and wraps around.

## The Placement

**1. The "Fortress Wall" (West Side - Direct Sow)**
- **Location:** Plant a **thick, staggered row** of sunflowers **15-20 feet West of the bus**, running parallel to the long side.
- **Purpose:** **Primary Windbreak.** This wall will take the brunt of the 25+ mph gusts before they hit your precious Waffle Gardens (which are located North/East of the bus).
- **Variety:** Use your **tallest, sturdiest** seeds here (Mammoth Russian or Skyscraper). They will get battered, and that's their job. They are the **sacrificial guardians**.
- **Spacing:** Direct sow seeds **6 inches apart**. Thin to 12-18 inches. We want a dense thicket, not a sparse row. The goal is a living fence that slows wind speed by 50-70%.

**2. The "Dappled Shade" (East Side - Pots or Direct Sow)**
- **Location:** Plant a **single, less-dense row** about **5-8 feet East of the bus**, or strategically place your friends' **potted sunflowers** here.
- **Purpose:** **Morning Sun Filter.** The East side gets glorious morning sun but can get hot by 11 AM. A tall sunflower here casts a **moving shadow** that will gently cool the Waffle Gardens during the critical transition from morning to midday.
- **Variety:** Use **branching, multi-headed varieties** (like Lemon Queen or Autumn Beauty). They are prettier for the kids to look at and provide more flowers for pollinators over a longer season.
- **Benefit:** This is the "Beauty & Pollinator" zone. The bees will follow the sunflowers here and then find your cucumbers and okra.

**3. The "Wind-Tunnel Plug" (Northwest Corner - Direct Sow)**
- **Location:** Plant a **tight cluster** of sunflowers directly at the **Northwest corner of the bus**.
- **Purpose:** **Turbulence Diffuser.** As wind hits the front face of the bus and screams around the corner, it creates a mini-whirlwind that can flatten seedlings. A dense sunflower clump here acts like a shock absorber, breaking up that energy.

**4. Where NOT to Plant**
- **DO NOT** plant sunflowers directly **South** of the bus. This is the "Oven Zone." The bus radiates 120°F+ heat from its metal skin in August. Sunflowers there will cook and reflect even more heat onto the back of the bus where you might want to rest.
- **DO NOT** plant sunflowers **immediately adjacent to the Olla Waffles**. Sunflowers are thirsty. If they are within 3 feet of a Waffle basin, their roots will find the Olla moisture gradient and out-compete your vegetables for that precious water. Keep them in their own designated dryland zones.

## Cohorts' Transplants

- **Week 5+:** Once the direct-sown wall is 2 feet tall, move the pots to the **East side** for the "Dappled Shade" effect or cluster them near the **entry path** so your 6-year-old can see the bees working at her eye level.

## Summary

| **Zone** | **Type** | **Distance from Bus** | **Function** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Far West Row** | Direct Sow (Tall) | 15-20 ft West | **Windbreak Fortress** |
| **NW Corner Clump** | Direct Sow (Tall) | 5 ft from corner | **Turbulence Diffuser** |
| **East Row** | Pots or Branching | 5-8 ft East | **Morning Shade & Pollinators** |
| **South Side** | **NOTHING** | 0 ft | **Radiant Heat Zone of Death** |

Direct sow in the ground **now**. The faster sunflowers establish a taproot, the sooner they start protecting the land. "Lemon Queen" seeds in pots are perfect for kids.
